# Governance

Bedriox/RakNet is developed and maintained by the [Bedriox](https://bedriox.com) team. The Bedriox maintainers are the project stewards and final decision makers.

Maintainers review code, releases, security reports, scope, and compatibility. Routine decisions are made through reviewed pull requests. Significant architecture, compatibility, governance, or security changes require a documented RFC in Bedriox/RFCs.

Maintainers must disclose relevant conflicts of interest, apply project rules consistently, preserve attribution, and prioritize project security. Roles and a broader community decision process may be introduced before or after public release as contributor participation grows.
