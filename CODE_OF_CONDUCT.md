# Code of Conduct

Bedriox contributors must create a professional, respectful, and harassment-free environment. Treat differing experience and viewpoints constructively; critique work rather than people; and do not publish private information or engage in discriminatory, threatening, sexual, or persistently disruptive conduct.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Bedriox team through the contact method published at [bedriox.com](https://bedriox.com). Reports will be reviewed confidentially to the extent practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
